# Lab log, from 2026-10-05

After Aaron's hardware pass of the merged site he asked for the Coil's method again: small lab pages with sliders, tuned by hand, before anything is specified or built. This file records what each lab is, what he has picked, what the designers recommended, and what a rollout has to watch for. Nothing here is shipped. Update it when he rules on a lab; move a ruling into `docs/design-decisions-2026-09-28.md` when it becomes a build.

Why labs: `docs/waveform-build-log-2026-10-05.md`, "Status after Aaron's hardware pass".

## How to run them

The labs live on the local branch `lab` (never merged), in a worktree beside the repo, as dev-only routes under `app/lab/` (`app/lab/layout.tsx` returns 404 outside `pnpm dev`). The launch config `lab` serves them at `http://localhost:3210`. Each lab has a collapsible panel, named presets with the designer's recommendation first, a theme switch, and a "copy values" button that puts the settings on the clipboard as JSON. Aaron pastes that JSON back as his decision.

Trial font files in `app/fonts/` are gitignored and were copied into the lab worktree by hand; the type lab does not compile without them.

| Lab | Route | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Type | `/lab/type` | Which face, weight, size and color carries the small non-body text |
| Wave | `/lab/wave` | How the soundtrack waveform lives in the background |
| Controls | `/lab/controls` | The hover fill for buttons, the Coil and Band toggle, the music note |
| Mark | `/lab/mark` | The logo Easter egg: the bolt strikes and makes the A, and a card about the mark |
| Gallery | `/lab/gallery` | The modal's photos: interleaved rows on desktop, a tap-or-swipe stage on phones, timed mask-ins |
| Footer | `/lab/footer` | The giant "build.stuff" wordmark with the field ending in it |

**Found on the real site while building the lab (fix with the typography change, not before):**

1. No loaded face has the "←" character. Profa lacks it and Inter is loaded with a subset that skips it, so every browser draws the back link's arrow from the system font. Replace it with a drawn icon (lucide `ArrowLeft`, 14px, stroke 2.5 beside Profa Bold).
2. In `components/soundtrack/BandInvite.tsx` the stacked control layers are not baseline-aligned: "Pause" and "Resume" sit 1px under the heading's baseline at 1440 wide and 3px at 1024. `items-baseline` on the controls grid fixes it.
3. Profa sits about 0.07em higher in its line box than Inter, so an icon centred on the line reads 1px low beside Profa text. Either metric overrides on the font loader or a 1px lift on icon and label pairs.
4. Small text has no size scale: 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14px are all in use. Weights do not follow role either ("Pause" is regular, the pill's "Paused" is medium).
5. The playback pill and player card set family and sizes inline, so a token change does not reach them.
6. The trial cuts stamp `*`, `;` and `@`; the same unicode-range workaround as `lib/fonts.ts` applies to any new Profa cut. Shipping Profa Bold needs its file allowlisted in `.gitignore`, as Black is.

## Wave lab

**The problem (Aaron).** The merged wave ducks under text, which he never wanted; its motion is confusing; it is not where he wants it.

**Round 1.** Five placements (backdrop, bottom strip, seams between sections, behind each section, side rail) and five self-contained loops. None was it.

**Round 2, the current direction (Aaron's reference: 21st.dev "svg follow scroll").** One curve wanders down the page behind the text, in flow, and scroll draws it: the head advances as the reader scrolls and retreats on the way back. Ours is the dotted waveform bent along the curve. Placement "Path" in the lab.

- Four spines: "Knot and sweep" (loops beside the band's question, then sweeps that leave both edges), "Through the words" (diagonals behind every text block), "Switchback" (sloped runs across each gap, turning off screen), "Calm S" (between the gutters).
- Spines are defined relative to the page: each point sits inside a section or a gap by fractions, measured from the `data-wave-avoid` boxes, so the curve stretches when copy changes. Adding or removing a section means editing the spines that name it.
- Designer's pick: preset "Unspool" (Knot and sweep, everything behind the head stays drawn, head at 62 percent of the viewport, 8 percent drawn before any scroll, smoothing 7 per second). Second: "Through the words" as a 1600px train that enters and leaves the screen.
- Tight bends: amplitude tapers with curvature so dots never fold. Self-crossings: one fill per color, so alpha never doubles.
- Rendering: in-flow canvas tiles no taller than 768px, only visible tiles repainted, nothing repainted at rest. Never a fixed canvas chasing scroll.
- Contrast with the wave behind text (Unspool): body text 13.4 to 1 light and 9.2 dark; muted text 3.6 light and 3.0 dark where a dot sits behind a letter. Holding muted text at 4.5 needs about 0.07 alpha, which is why the ducked wave was nearly invisible. Aaron's call.
- Not built: a phone variant (below about 1100px the gutter spines cross text and sweeps steepen).

**Aaron's pick, round 2** (his copied JSON, 2026-10-05): Path is the direction; "the scroll reveal implementation is looking great". The "Through the words" spine as a train: `tail: train`, `trainLength: 1600`, `headMode: viewport`, `headAt: 0.7`, `preDrawn: 0`, `smoothing: 7`, `headStyle: swell`, `wavelength: 240`, `shapeTravel: 0`; music on at intensity 0.8 with the beat, `musicLayer: 0.35`; alphas muted 0.35 light and 0.34 dark, accent 0.5 both; amplitude 80, thickness cap 5, spacing 13, edge fade 0.24. Knot and sweep ("Unspool") is parked: too messy.

**Round 3, building:** he liked both Through the words and Switchback and wants one less uniform line, some arcs behind the words and some off screen, "a bit more of a random aspect" that is "more personal": irregular authored spines plus a seeded generator, with the open question of one shape for everyone or a new one each visit. And the cursor: the real band's wave parts around the pointer (`carveTargets` in `lib/waveform/dots.ts`); the lab never had it, and he wants it and other designs (a speed-proportional push like the hero name's wake, a pluck that ripples along the line, a swell, a brighten) on the path.

## Controls lab

**The ask (Aaron).** A hover fill for the Menu, the playback pill and other buttons, after the "arrow fill button" by Hyperiux on 21st.dev; a better music note, keeping a note because it is personal; and the coming Coil and Band toggle.

- The fill is a house primitive, not the reference's code (that is Tailwind 4, viewport-sized, hard-coded colors). One registered custom property runs 0 to 1 on hover, focus-visible or a touch press; the control's content is rendered twice and the top copy is clipped by the same value, so the fill and the label's color change cannot drift. Reduced motion gets an instant change.
- Variants: the reference's circle and arrow; grows from the control's icon; a directional wipe; the underline rises into the fill; grows from the pointer.
- Designer's pick, preset "House fill": icon pills fill from their icon, underlined links from their underline, and only the real calls to action get the circle and arrow. 450ms on the reference's curve.
- Coil and Band toggle: a 40px two-half capsule on the Menu pill's frosted surface; the accent fill is the selection, and on a switch the leading edge goes first and the trailing edge follows about 100ms later.
- Notes: eight drawings beside the current `NoteIcon`. Designer's pick: the solid eighth. Personal option: an eighth whose flag is cut like the bolt mark. Animation pick: Aaron's recorded idea applied to the note, a small x when off, then the x shrinks and two arcs arrive, about 200ms.

**Open with Aaron:** which fill, note and animation; whether blur may extend to a hero control (Layer 1 allows it only on modals, the Menu pill and the playback pill; a solid surface is the fallback); where the toggle sits. "Work and photos" was removed from the hero on 2026-09-29 (decision 2.10), so the toggle would be the hero's first visible control since then.

**Rollout cautions:**

- The Menu pill is two buttons in one shape; the Listen dot can fill only its own slot, and the pill's 500ms odometer must move onto the fill's clock.
- `PlaybackPill`, `PillParts` and `PlayerCard` style almost everything inline, and the label slot's width animation runs inside content that would be duplicated; the copy needs `aria-hidden` and `inert`.
- The 44px hit areas come from pseudo-elements and must not be clipped: no `overflow: hidden` on the control.
- "See more" in the work modal would grow from a text link into a 44px pill.
- Animating a registered custom property needs Chrome, Safari 16.4 or Firefox 128 and up; older browsers get the instant change.

**Aaron's pick, 2026-10-06 (his copied JSON, preset "custom"):** the hover fill ships: 450ms on the reference in-out circ curve, from the start edge, 10px fill radius, the arrow swap on, reduced motion an instant colour change. Per control: Menu pill and the playback capsule "icon" fill with glass filling to accent; nav bar links and the Connect links "rise" with the quiet tint; the band's controls "rise" with glass to accent; the hero control and the calls to action "circle" with glass to accent; the hero control sits on the Menu pill's glass with an 8px blur.
